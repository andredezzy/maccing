## 5. Conversation-Based Pricing → Per-Message Pricing (July 2025)

### The Change

**Effective July 1, 2025:** Meta replaced Conversation-Based Pricing (CBP) with Per-Message Pricing (PMP).

- **Before:** One flat fee per 24-hour conversation window, regardless of how many messages
- **After:** Each **delivered** template message is billed individually
- Failed deliveries: not charged
- Service messages (free-form replies within CSW): free **until 1 October 2026**
- Utility templates sent within an open CSW: free **until 1 October 2026**

Source: [Pricing on the WhatsApp Business Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing).

### What Changes on 1 October 2026

Meta has announced the end of the free-CSW subsidy. Both remaining "free inside the window" categories become billable on the same date.

- **Service messages** — any non-template reply inside an open 24h CSW, whether typed by a human agent or generated by a third-party bot — are charged **per message** from 1 October 2026. They had been free since 1 November 2024.
- **Utility templates sent in response to a user inside an open 24h CSW** are charged **per message** from 1 October 2026. They had been free since 1 July 2025.
- **Rate:** service messages are priced at the **same per-market rate Meta charges for utility and authentication** templates.
- **No volume tiers on service messages.** Utility and authentication keep their tiers; service gets none. Service traffic does not get cheaper with scale, ever.
- **The 72-hour free entry point window is unchanged.** Messages inside an open FEP window (Click-to-WhatsApp ad or Facebook Page CTA origin) stay free for delivery.
- **October rates are not published yet.** Meta committed to publishing the rates effective 1 October 2026, service rates included, **no later than 1 September 2026**. Until then, budget service messages at the current utility/authentication rate for the destination market.
- Separately, **Meta Business Agent** replies (Meta's own agent platform, launched 1 July 2026) are charged **per token** from **1 August 2026**, at **$2.00 USD per 1M tokens** — roughly 20,000–25,000 tokens per message, so ~4–5 US cents each. A message is charged once: either as Meta Business Agent or as service, never both.

Source: [Pricing for non-template messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages) — Meta's own timeline, category table, and rates section.

**Planning consequence.** Any cost model that treats support replies and in-window order updates as free is valid for seven more weeks from 2026-08-10 and then wrong. Meta's own worked example: the identical one-hour conversation that carries 3 charges in August 2026 carries 5 from October.

### Free Messaging Windows

1. **24-hour Customer Service Window (CSW):** Opens when a user sends you any message **or makes/receives a call through the Calling API**. The window resets with each new inbound message or call from the user. Until 1 October 2026, all free-form messages and all utility templates sent within this window are free; from that date both are charged per message. Service conversations have been free with no monthly cap since **1 November 2024** (Meta removed the previous 1,000 service conversation/month cap on that date). Sources: [Pricing → Free non-template messages](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing#free-non-template-messages), [Pricing for non-template messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages).

2. **72-hour Entry Point Window:** When a user on **Android or iOS** initiates contact via a Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ad or Facebook Page CTA button, and the business responds within 24 hours, all messages to that user (including marketing templates) are **free for 72 hours**. Desktop and web WhatsApp clients do not trigger this window. Meta states explicitly that the FEP window is **unchanged** by the October 2026 update — messages inside it remain free for delivery, which makes it the last free-delivery window standing. Source: [Pricing → Free entry point windows](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing#free-entry-point-windows).

### Volume Tier Discounts (Utility & Authentication only)

Marketing receives **no volume discounts**, and **service messages will receive none either** once they become billable on 1 October 2026. Only utility and authentication tier.

How the tiers actually work ([Pricing → Volume tiers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing#volume-tiers)):

- Tiers are **market–category specific**. Brazil-utility and India-utility have different bands *and* different rates. There is no single global tier table.
- Volume is aggregated **across every WABA in the business portfolio**, per market–category pair.
- Only **charged** messages accrue. Utility templates delivered inside a CSW or inside an FEP window do not count toward tiers.
- Rates are **tier-specific**: crossing a threshold prices only the messages in that band, not the month retroactively.
- Tiers **reset monthly** at 12am WABA timezone. An `account_update` webhook with `event: VOLUME_BASED_PRICING_TIER_UPDATE` fires on each tier change.

### Cost Optimization Strategies

Sorted by whether the tactic survives 1 October 2026.

**Survives the October change:**

1. **Drive conversations through CTWA / Page CTA for the 72-hour free entry point.** Meta states the FEP window is unchanged: for 72 hours after you respond to an ad- or CTA-initiated conversation, every message type — marketing templates included — is free for delivery. After October this is the *only* remaining free-delivery window, which makes it the single highest-leverage cost lever you have.
2. **Say more per message.** Service billing is per message, not per character (body limit 1,024 characters). Three short replies cost 3×; one composed reply costs 1×. Same for bot flows — collect name, order ID and issue in one interactive step, not three.
3. **Prefer a template where the content is predictable.** Utility and authentication keep volume tiers; service never gets them. At volume, a structured utility template is cheaper per message than the equivalent free-form reply.
4. **Segment and personalize.** Generic broadcasts drive block rates, which hurt quality rating, which limits throughput tiers and triggers template pacing.
5. **Clean your contact list.** You are charged per delivered message. Failed deliveries aren't charged, but sending to dead numbers wastes API calls and hurts quality metrics.
6. **Match category to actual content.** Meta reclassifies utility→marketing (you pay the higher rate); content that should be authentication is *rejected* outright the following month, with no category review available. See `templates.md` → Template Strategy.
7. **Fund the wallet per-day, never bulk (disposable/free-tier BSP):** Top up only the current day's send budget plus a small buffer each day, immediately before sending — never pre-fund a multi-day ramp. A suspended or risk-flagged BSP account can freeze a large prepaid balance. Full doctrine: `meta` skill → `disposable-bm-strategy.md` (Number Warming Protocol → "Wallet funding").

**Expires 30 September 2026 — do not carry these into a 2027 model:**

- *"Trigger an inbound message to open a free 24-hour window before sending utility content."* From 1 October the CSW no longer makes utility templates free. An open window still buys you the right to send non-template messages at all — but at the utility/authentication rate.
- *"Batch all utility info into the free window."* Batching still helps, because fewer messages means fewer charges. The window itself is no longer the reason.

### Pricing Model Notes

**What you pay has three independent layers:**

1. **Meta's per-message charge** — set by Meta, same for all BSPs, destination-based (recipient country calling code, not sender). Updates quarterly. Brazil marketing ~$0.0625/msg is among the world's highest.
2. **BSP platform/subscription fee** — monthly or annual, ranges from $0 (YCloud Free, AiSensy Free, Gupshup self-serve, Twilio PAYG) to thousands for enterprise CPaaS.
3. **BSP per-message markup** — ranges from 0% (YCloud, Respond.io, Zoko Plus+, Bird, AiSensy claimed, Trengo, 360dialog via MM API) to ~25%+ (Wati ~20%, Interakt ~25%, Zenvia opaque, Take Blip ~100%+ list price).

**What is free today — and only until 1 October 2026 for the first two:**
- Service messages (non-template replies within an open 24h window): $0, no cap, since Nov 1 2024. **Charged per message from 1 Oct 2026**, at the market's utility/authentication rate, with no volume tier.
- Utility templates sent *within* an open 24h customer-service window: $0 since Jul 1 2025. **Charged per message from 1 Oct 2026.**
- All messages for 72h after a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or Facebook Page CTA. **Unchanged** by the October update.

**Brazil-specific:**
- BRL-native billing from Meta went live **1 July 2026** for Solution Partners and directly-integrated clients whose Sold-To country is Brazil in Meta Billing Hub; those accounts are invoiced in BRL by Facebook Brasil. Everyone else is still invoiced in USD. Eligible customers must migrate all WABAs to BRL by 30 June 2027.
- Brazilian-local BSPs (Take Blip, Zenvia, SocialHub.pro) already bill in BRL with NF-e — relevant for LGPD compliance and Brazilian accounting.
- Import taxes (ISS, IRRF, CIDE, PIS/COFINS-Importação, IOF) apply to USD remittances from Brazil; BRL-native billing eliminates this complexity.
- **Empirically confirmed in production (YCloud):** service messages = totalPrice:0; Brazil marketing draws from wallet at ~$0.05–$0.0625/msg — consistent with Meta's $0.0625 base at 0% markup.

**MM API vs Cloud API (360dialog / Gupshup):** Meta's Marketing Messages (MM) API route can eliminate BSP surcharges that apply only on the standard `/messages` Cloud API endpoint. If using 360dialog or Gupshup for high-volume Brazil marketing, prefer the MM API/MM Lite route.

**Meta per-message rate (Brazil, verified 2026-08-10):** Marketing $0.0625/msg, utility and authentication $0.0068/msg (per-message billing since July 2025, was per-conversation). Service (non-template, inside the 24h window) free **until 1 October 2026**, then charged at the utility/authentication rate.

### Why BSP over Direct API

**Why BSP over Direct API:** BSP uses THEIR developer app — you never need a Meta developer account. This bypasses the SMS verification blocker that affects purchased/antidetect profiles. The Embedded Signup flow only requires Facebook login + BM admin access.

**Direct Cloud API without your own dev account (workaround):** A SECOND Facebook account (contractor/employee with working phone) registers as developer → creates a "Business" Meta App → adds it to your BM → you (BM admin) create a System User → install the app → generate token with `whatsapp_business_messaging`. The token generation itself does NOT need dev SMS verification — only the app creation does, which the second account handles. Cheapest long-term (zero markup), keeps existing WABA, full script control.
